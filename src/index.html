IITH Quest – an 8-bit IIT Hyderabad campus platformer

IITH Quest is a free retro platformer about student life at IIT Hyderabad. Play as a boy or girl through 8 levels, from orientation day to convocation: catch credits, beat assignment and exam bosses, pet friendly campus dogs, ride cycles and land a placement offer. Your CGPA goes on the global Hall of Fame. Plays in any browser on phone or desktop.

DRAG THE STICK OR A/B TO MOVE THEM

Arrows / WASD move · Space or Z jump · X or Shift dash · P pause
